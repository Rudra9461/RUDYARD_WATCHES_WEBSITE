Icon of the Year

Rolex Submariner

Widely regarded as one of the premier luxury watch brands globally, Rolex has a reputation for luxury and exclusivity. The Submariner is an iconic dive watch — water-resistant to 300m, featuring a unidirectional bezel and the legendary Oyster bracelet.

Whether worn for a dive or a formal event, the Submariner's combination of style, durability, and functionality makes it a must-have for any watch enthusiast.

Who We Are

About Rudyard Watches

Rudyard Watches was founded with one belief — a great watch is not bought, it is chosen. We are a curated men's watch destination bringing together the finest timepieces from global and Indian brands, all under one roof.

From the precision engineering of Rolex to the bold smart-tech of boAt, we source watches that match every personality, occasion, and budget. Our team personally tests and reviews every model before it earns a place in our collection.

Based in Jaipur, Rajasthan — we serve watch enthusiasts across India, with a promise of authenticity, transparency, and timeless style.
